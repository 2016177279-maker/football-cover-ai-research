Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Football Cover AI Research is an independent, non-commercial, nonprofit academic research project.

Data Used

The project uses the YouTube Data API to obtain publicly available data needed for research. The research covers publicly available video metadata and publicly accessible video thumbnails or cover images.

The project does not require members of the public to sign in with a Google or YouTube account. It does not obtain or store Google user passwords, OAuth credentials, private YouTube data, or other non-public Google user information.

The project does not collect personal information directly from end users.

Purpose and Use

Data obtained through the YouTube Data API is used only for academic research, statistical analysis, and validation of research methods.

The project does not sell data obtained through the YouTube API and does not use such data for advertising, profiling, or commercial resale.

Data Retention

Public YouTube metadata and thumbnail information are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to conduct and validate the research.

Football Cover AI Research is not currently a commercial software-as-a-service (SaaS) product offered to the public.
