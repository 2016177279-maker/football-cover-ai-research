# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 17, 2026**

Football Cover AI Research is an independent, non-commercial, nonprofit academic research project.

## Data Used

The project uses the YouTube Data API to obtain public data needed for research. The research covers publicly available video metadata and publicly accessible video thumbnails or cover images.

The project does not require members of the public to sign in with a Google or YouTube account. It does not obtain or store Google user passwords, OAuth credentials, or private YouTube data.

## Purpose and Use

Data obtained through the YouTube Data API is used only for academic research, statistical analysis, and validation of research methods. The project does not sell data obtained through the YouTube API.

Football Cover AI Research is not currently a commercial software-as-a-service (SaaS) product offered to the public.
