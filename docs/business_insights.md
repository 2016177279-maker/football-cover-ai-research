# Business Insights

## From Model Output to Decision Support

The purpose of the analysis is not to report coefficients in isolation. The project translates statistically and operationally meaningful evidence into practical content-design guidance while preserving uncertainty and avoiding causal overclaiming.

## Selected Associations

Representative modeled associations for an interquartile-range change:

- **Aesthetic coherence:** +28.2% modeled Velocity association (95% CI +16.5% to +41.0%).
- **Text coverage:** -23.6% (95% CI -30.4% to -16.2%).
- **Saturation:** +18.5% (95% CI +4.5% to +34.3%), with mixed machine-learning evidence.
- **Visual entropy:** -13.6% (95% CI -21.4% to -5.1%).

These values are associations under the modeled specification, not guaranteed causal uplifts.

## Decision Translation

### 1. Reduce excessive text burden
High text coverage is associated with lower modeled Velocity. The practical implication is to prioritize one clear message instead of competing blocks of text.

**Design rule:** simplify redundant text while preserving the factual title meaning.

### 2. Improve aesthetic coherence
Aesthetic coherence shows one of the strongest positive modeled associations among the selected visual variables.

**Design rule:** improve visual consistency, hierarchy and composition without changing the underlying football event or factual claim.

### 3. Reduce avoidable visual complexity
Higher visual entropy is associated with lower modeled Velocity.

**Design rule:** remove competing micro-elements and unnecessary clutter while keeping context that is needed to understand the content.

### 4. Use saturation cautiously
Saturation shows a positive regression association, but evidence is less consistent across modeling approaches.

**Design rule:** do not treat higher saturation as a universal optimization target; use it only when it supports the content and visual hierarchy.

## Heterogeneity

The project does not assume that one thumbnail rule works equally well for every football content type. Player, club, competition and other topic categories can differ in visual structure and audience behavior.

For this reason, the final recommendation logic is designed to support context-aware intervention selection rather than a single universal thumbnail template.

## Business Value

The analytical workflow demonstrates how unstructured visual content can be converted into structured business evidence:

**Image → Features → Statistical Evidence → Interpretable Insight → Design Rule → AI-assisted Candidate → Human Decision**

This is the core business-analytics contribution of the project.
