# Synthetic examples

These two small CSV files are manually constructed fixtures for documentation,
unit tests, and the offline Streamlit proof of concept.

- `synthetic_videos.csv` contains 12 fictional videos and channels.
- `synthetic_features.csv` contains corresponding fictional visual measurements.

Identifiers beginning with `demo_video_` and `demo_channel_` do not refer to
real YouTube records. The rows were not sampled, transformed, or copied from the
5,200-record research dataset. Values are illustrative and must not be used to
reproduce or validate the reported research findings.
